300X3 Sale / Receipt Record

Internal operational form. The final contract and ledger state are managed by Corda. This form must not contain card numbers, CVV, bank credentials, private keys, or passwords.

1. Correlation and receipt
Use approved internal ID; do not enter payment credentials.
2. Product and serial identity
3. Payment and contract state
Use provider reference or hash, never card data.
Populate only after Corda confirmation.
4. Authorization and notes

Finalization rule: the receipt is provisional until the Corda transaction is confirmed and its transaction ID/state are written back to the PostgreSQL sales projection.
