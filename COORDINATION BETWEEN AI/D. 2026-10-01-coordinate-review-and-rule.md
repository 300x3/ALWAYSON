## The standing coordinate rule — now permanent

The operator asked that "coordinate" be a general behaviour, not a one-off. It is
now a Cline rule, written to all three mirrors the project already uses:

```
~/.cline/rules/10-coordinate-protocol.md            (loaded by Cline)
~/.agents/10-coordinate-protocol.md                  (agent-generic)
~/Documents/Cline/Rules/10-coordinate-protocol.md    (operator copy)
```

It encodes, for any future session told to *coordinate*:

- read every same-day document first; claim the **next unused letter**; never
  renumber, edit or delete another session's file;
- **verify before asserting** — re-measure numbers, because they go stale within
  the hour (this document exists because they did);
- the required document structure, and an instruction to record what you got
  wrong and *why*;
- **stage by file, never `git add -A`** in a shared tree;
- the load-bearing invariants (flat Quadlet deploy, masked units are often
  deliberate, no secret values, no "fixed" without a verification command).
