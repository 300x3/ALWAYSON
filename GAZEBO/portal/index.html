ao-sim-fabrication — Gazebo factory world

Operator surface for the fabrication simulation domain (README §10.2.1). This portal operates the simulation only and is never a control path to live machinery (§4.3). Scope and honesty

  • Every boning datum in sim/boning.yaml is derived from the SketchUp export AABBs, not surveyed on the shop floor. The 3D printer and CNC bed are not in the exports at all.
  • The portal therefore reports reach_verified_against_machine as false until an operator declares those frames. A simulated reach must not be called verified against a real envelope before then.
  • The Gazebo 3D view is a separate client (ao-sim-fabrication-gui-gz), started on demand. It is not embedded in this page.
